# Official network routes

These programmes provide existing routes for support, collaboration or governed data access. Their eligibility and consent rules apply independently of Zebratlas.

| Network | Route |
| --- | --- |
| [EURORDIS](https://eurordis.org) | European patient alliance; also listed in [RDI's member directory](https://www.rarediseasesinternational.org/members-list/). |
| [Rare Diseases International](https://www.rarediseasesinternational.org/members-list/) | Find patient organisations across countries. |
| [IRDiRC](https://irdirc.org/why-we-exist/) | International rare-disease research coordination. |
| [Global Genes / RARE-X](https://rare-x.org/) | Community support and patient-led research programmes; [research access](https://rare-x.org/researchers-access/) follows its consent and access process. |
| [NORD](https://rarediseases.org/contact/) | US patient and organisation enquiries. |
| [Orphanet](https://www.orpha.net/en/institutions/get-in-touch) | National expert-resource and institutional enquiries. |
| [Matchmaker Exchange](https://www.matchmakerexchange.org/participants.html) | Participating matching services with their own user and submission rules. |
| [ELIXIR Rare Diseases Community](https://elixir-europe.org/communities/rare-diseases) | Research infrastructure, tools and authorised data access. |

Follow each programme's secure process for patient or genetic data.
